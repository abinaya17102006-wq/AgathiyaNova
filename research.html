A practical framework

From idea to research project

1

Choose a focused question

Identify a specific topic in Siddha medicine, medicinal botany or integrative health that can be explored responsibly.

2

Review existing literature

Search relevant databases, compare sources and record the authors, publication year and study type.

3

Evaluate the evidence

Distinguish laboratory studies, animal studies, observational research and human clinical trials. One study alone rarely establishes effectiveness.

4

Plan your methodology

Define your objective, study design, materials, data-collection method and limitations before beginning.

5

Write and cite responsibly

Use your own words, cite original sources and report results honestly, including limitations and unexpected findings.

Research integrity matters

Do not present traditional use as proven clinical effectiveness without suitable evidence. Human or animal research may require institutional ethics approval. Never test a medicinal preparation on yourself or others as an informal experiment.
